### Question & Answer

1. What is a dictionary?
Answer: A mutable mapping of unique hashable keys to values.

2. What makes a valid dictionary key?
Answer: It must be hashable.

3. get vs indexing.
Answer: get can return a default/None for missing keys; indexing raises KeyError.

4. What does update() do?
Answer: It adds or replaces entries using another mapping or iterable of pairs.

5. How do you iterate over keys and values?
Answer: Use d.items() to iterate over key-value pairs.

6. Can values be duplicated?
Answer: Yes, dictionary values do not need to be unique.

7. What is dictionary comprehension?
Answer: A compact syntax for constructing dictionaries from an iterable.

8. How do you delete a dictionary entry?
Answer: Use del, pop(), or popitem(), depending on the desired behavior.
